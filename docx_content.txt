A2. Biography — Updated Tab Body
📌 Replace the current Biography paragraph with this text.
Vikas Jindal is a Professor in the Department of Metallurgical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (Banaras Hindu University), Varanasi. He holds a Ph.D. in Metallurgical Engineering from IIT(BHU), an M.S. from the Singapore-MIT Alliance (National University of Singapore), and a B.Tech. from IIT Kanpur. Before joining IIT(BHU), he worked as an Engineer at Chartered Semiconductor Manufacturing Ltd., Singapore; as a Project Assistant at IIT Kanpur; as a Quick Hire Fellow at the National Metallurgical Laboratory, Jamshedpur; and as an Assistant Professor at PEC University of Technology, Chandigarh. With extensive experience in academia and industry spanning over 20 years, his research focuses on computational thermodynamics, machine learning in materials design, and the development of models and databases for advanced materials applications. He is an Editorial Board Member of Scientific Reports (Nature Portfolio Journal) since June 2025.

Key paragraph to add under ML section:
Recent work from the MML Group integrates machine learning with DFT and experimental data to predict materials properties in high-entropy alloys. A neural network model was developed to characterize the interplay between short-range ordering and enthalpy of mixing in the NbTiVZr system (J. Phase Equilibria Diffus., 2023). More recently, an ML+DFT approach was demonstrated for Vickers hardness prediction in refractory HEAs (MRS Communications, 2025; Intermetallics, 2026). These efforts position the group at the intersection of traditional computational thermodynamics and modern data-driven materials science.
