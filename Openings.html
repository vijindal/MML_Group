Openings

The Materials Modeling Lab provides a dynamic research environment at the intersection of computational thermodynamics, machine learning, and advanced materials design. We welcome candidates at all levels.

Post-Doctoral Positions

We periodically invite applications for Post-Doctoral Fellows through the IIT(BHU) Institute Post-Doctoral Fellowship (IPDF) scheme. The current round is closed. Announcements for the next round will be posted here.

Interested candidates may write to vjindal.met@iitbhu.ac.in to express their interest in advance.

PhD Positions

We invite applications from motivated PhD candidates with a strong background in materials science, metallurgy, physics, or computational engineering. Current research areas include:

  • Computational Thermodynamics (CALPHAD, CVM, CE methods)
  • Machine Learning for Materials Property Prediction and Alloy Design
  • Ti-TiB Functionally Graded Materials and Composites for Defence/Biomedical
  • Beta-Titanium Alloys for Biomedical Implant Applications
  • Atomistic Simulations (MD/MC) for Alloy Thermodynamics and Phase Transitions
  • High Entropy Alloys: Thermodynamic Modelling and Short-Range Ordering

How to Apply

  1. Apply through the IIT(BHU) admissions portal: iitbhu.ac.in/dean/doaa/admission/pg
  2. Email your CV to Dr. Vikas Jindal at vjindal.met@iitbhu.ac.in mentioning your area of interest.

M.Tech & B.Tech Projects

Multiple project slots are available for IIT(BHU) students at the M.Tech and B.Tech (IDD) level. Current project topics include:

  • Monte Carlo simulations for alloy thermodynamics and short-range ordering
  • SQS-based DFT calculations for multicomponent alloy systems
  • CALPHAD database development for novel material systems
  • Machine learning models for HEA hardness and property prediction
  • Synthesis and characterisation of Ti-TiB composites via vacuum arc melting / hot pressing
  • Thermodynamic assessment of ternary/quaternary systems

Interested students should contact Dr. Jindal directly at vjindal.met@iitbhu.ac.in.
