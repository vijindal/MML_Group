Openings

The Materials Modeling Lab provides a dynamic research environment at the intersection of computational thermodynamics, machine learning, and advanced materials design. We welcome candidates at all levels.

Post-Doctoral Positions

We periodically invite applications for Post-Doctoral Fellows through the IIT(BHU) Institute Post-Doctoral Fellowship (IPDF) scheme. The current round is closed. Announcements for the next round will be posted here.

Interested candidates may write to vjindal.met@iitbhu.ac.in to express their interest in advance.

PhD and M.Tech Admissions

We welcome applications from motivated candidates for PhD and M.Tech (Research) programs in the following research areas:

  • Computational Thermodynamics (CALPHAD, CVM, CE methods)
  • Machine Learning for Materials Property Prediction and Alloy Design
  • Ti-TiB Functionally Graded Materials and Composites for Defence and Biomedical Applications
  • Beta-Titanium Alloys for Biomedical Implant Applications
  • Atomistic Simulations (MD/MC) for Alloy Thermodynamics and Phase Transitions
  • High Entropy Alloys: Thermodynamic Modelling and Short-Range Ordering

Eligibility (Metallurgical Engineering, PhD)

As per the IIT (BHU) PG/PhD admission brochure, eligible qualifications for PhD in Metallurgical Engineering include:

  • Bachelor's / Master's degree in Mechanical / Chemical / Production Engineering / Manufacturing Engineering / Mineral Engineering / Ceramic Engineering
  • Master's degree in Materials Science / Engineering / Technology
  • Master's degree in Physical Sciences (Solid State Physics) / Chemical Sciences (Inorganic / Physical Chemistry / Industrial Chemistry) / Biological Sciences / Geology, with Mathematics as a subject at Bachelor's level

Please refer to the official IIT(BHU) admission brochure for complete and updated eligibility, category-wise seats, and selection criteria.
